## What this status applies to

The OpenAI Select Partner status applies to **OMS IT as an organization**.

It does **not** automatically mean that any individual OMS IT product, BD AI, OMS Nexus, customer project, application, website, service, course, book, or other initiative is separately:

- approved by OpenAI;
- certified by OpenAI;
- sponsored by OpenAI;
- endorsed by OpenAI;
- operated by OpenAI; or
- authorized to represent itself as an OpenAI service.

Any separate relationship must be stated only when authoritative evidence supports that exact claim.

## Learning credentials are separate

Individual OpenAI Academy courses, practitioner credentials, training completions, or other learning records are separate from OMS IT's organization-level partner status.

A personal learning credential should not be used as the sole evidence for an organizational partnership claim.
